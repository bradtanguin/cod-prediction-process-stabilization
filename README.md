# Machine learning-based prediction of influent Chemical Oxygen Demand (COD) for Food-to-Microorganism (F/M) ratio estimation and Sludge Propagation Optimization in Wastewater Treatment

## 📌 Background

<p align="justify"> The stabilization of Biotank 4 requires monitoring influent characteristics, particularly Chemical Oxygen Demand (COD), to assess organic loading and estimate the Food-to-Microorganism (F/M) ratio [1]. This project applies Machine Learning (ML) in order to predict influent COD and support F/M ratio estimation.


<p align="justify"> This prediction will provide insights for making data-driven decisions in monitoring Mixed Liquor Suspended Solids (MLSS), Mixed Liquor Volatile Suspended Solids (MLVSS), Sludge Volume Index (SVI), and 30-minute Settled Sludge Volume (SV30) to improve Biological Nutrient Removal (BNR) performance.

## ❗ Problem Statement

<p align="justify"> During the stabilization phase of newly commissioned Biotank 4, laboratory monitoring was constrained by the limited availability of analytical reagents required for COD and other multiparameter analyses. The delay was attributable to logistical and regulatory constraints affecting the procurement and distribution; moreover, COD analysis could only be performed on a limited number of occasions during the one-month sludge propagation period. As a result, these limitations reduced the frequency of laboratory data available for operational monitoring.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

This project aims to develop a data-driven approach for predicting influent COD and supporting the monitoring and assessment of Biotank 4 during its 30-day stabilization period through the following objectives:

- To clean and preprocess wastewater treatment data.
- To develop and evaluate Machine Learning models for influent Chemical Oxygen Demand (COD) prediction.
- To validate predicted COD values against laboratory results and support Food-to-Microorganism (F/M) ratio estimation.
- To provide data-driven insights for monitoring Mixed Liquor Suspended Solids (MLSS), Mixed Liquor Volatile Suspended Solids (MLVSS), Sludge Volume Index (SVI), and 30-minute Settled Sludge Volume (SV30) to support improved Biological Nutrient Removal (BNR) performance.
